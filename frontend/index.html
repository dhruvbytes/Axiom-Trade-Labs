MUST READ BEFORE CHANGING SETTINGS

These settings change how the autonomous trading system behaves. They do not remove the system's built-in safety protections.

  • Every successful Apply will temporarily pause NEW trading decisions for 60 seconds.
  • This pause is intentional. It gives the system a short safety window after a policy change.
  • Your brokerage account is NOT frozen. Existing orders and already-authorized activity are NOT cancelled.
  • Market data and monitoring continue running. After the safety window ends, the system resumes normal operations.
  • Moving sliders does NOT trigger the pause. The pause happens only after you successfully press Apply.
  • Reset to Defaults restores original project settings and also starts the same 60-second safety pause.
  • Setting something more aggressive does NOT guarantee a trade. The system can still choose NO TRADE when conditions are unsafe.

How Axiom Works

A transparent look at how Axiom combines AI reasoning with deterministic financial controls.

ARCHITECTURAL OVERVIEW

Axiom Trade Labs is a hybrid trading system. AI and analytical components help interpret intent, context and market opportunities, while a deterministic financial core controls risk, admission and execution.

DUAL-MODE HYBRID ARCHITECTURE

You are never locked into a single workflow. Axiom combines an Autonomous Engine (for hands-free, continuous market scanning and execution) with an interactive AI Trading Assistant (via the Trading Agent chat drawer). SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & FAQ

Axiom Trade Labs is a dual-mode hybrid trading platform. It gives you complete flexibility: you can use the background Autonomous Engine for hands-free, rules-driven execution across market regimes, or you can interact directly with the AI Trading Assistant via chat for portfolio queries and analysis. Both the automated engine and the manual assistant operate under the exact same strict, deterministic risk and safety core.

Decisions follow a strict multi-stage pipeline: Market Event or user intent → Context Snapshot → Hypothesis Evaluation (e.g., Trend Continuation, Mean Reversion, Defensive Reduction) → Contextual Selection → Proposal Generation → Shared Admission → Risk Engine evaluation → CORE-X execution. If safety criteria are not met, the system chooses NO_TRADE.

SCSV is the deterministic routing layer responsible for understanding and mapping requests to appropriate tools and capabilities before actions reach execution. It performs entity identification, schema validation, and multi-intent routing without holding direct execution authority.

The Risk Engine is the primary financial safety boundary. It independently inspects every proposed trade against account balance, concentration limits, daily loss thresholds, and market data freshness. Proposals result in ALLOW, BLOCK, or REVIEW, enforcing strict fail-closed behavior when data is missing or stale.

CORE-X is the isolated execution boundary. Its sole responsibility is to translate approved risk-checked proposals into structured brokerage orders with strict idempotency and uncertainty handling. It does not evaluate strategy or overrule the Risk Engine.

VERIFIED MATHEMATICAL METHODS

• Risk Gate Calculations: Validates exposure limits and drawdown halts.
• Position Sizing: Dynamically calculates allowable shares/contracts from risk percentage.
• BMS / Price Boundary: Evaluates acceptable execution price limits and quote sanity.
• Bayesian Posterior Update: Adjusts strategy confidence weights based on observed outcomes.
• Contextual Strategy Scoring: Ranks eligible hypotheses against regime rules.
• Exposure & Concentration: Prevents over-allocation into single tickers.
• Option Contract Validation: Enforces spread and DTE bounds.
